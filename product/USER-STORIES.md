# Representative user stories

These are portfolio examples of intended product behavior, not independently verified live acceptance results.

## Owner-controlled outreach
As a business owner, I want to preview invoice follow-ups so that I retain control of communications.

Acceptance criteria: A message on review hold cannot be automatically sent; changed invoice or recipient data requires renewed approval; uncertain delivery is escalated rather than blindly retried.

## Separate acceptance from payment
As a business owner, I want invoice acceptance distinct from payment status so that a customer's approval does not appear as cleared funds.

Acceptance criteria: Updating acceptance must not mark an invoice paid; customer emails claiming payment need independent verification.

## Handle uncertainty
As a business owner, I want ambiguous responses routed for review so that no unsupported claim or promise is sent.

Acceptance criteria: Disputes, recipient changes, missing invoice evidence, and unclear replies create review tasks.

## Frequency controls
As a business owner, I want sending windows and limits so that follow-ups respect my communication preferences.

Acceptance criteria: Owner pause overrides automation; disallowed windows block scheduled sends.
