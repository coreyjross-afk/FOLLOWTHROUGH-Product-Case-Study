# Product decisions and tradeoffs

| Decision | Why | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Owner review for uncertain cases | Lower risk of inappropriate communications | More manual input |
| Acceptance distinct from payment | Avoid conflating approval with cleared funds | More states to track |
| Optional accounting connection | Support manual-invoice workflows | Greater need for payment-verification controls |
| Hold on ambiguous replies | Prevent unsupported commitments | More exceptions to resolve |
| Readiness checks for automation | Avoid false claims of enabled functionality | More setup complexity |

## Interview talking points

Discuss problem definition, MVP scope, user stories, acceptance criteria, AI-assisted implementation, testing versus live verification, and lessons from deployment troubleshooting. Avoid asserting measured ROI or customer adoption without evidence.
